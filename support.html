Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

One player creates a room and shares the code with friends. Once everyone has joined, the host starts the game: players take turns across different modes (Higher or Lower, The Impostor, Quick Answer, First to Last, Do You Trust Me?, Showdown).

I can't join a room

Double-check the room code and make sure everyone in the group has an active internet connection. If the problem persists, close and reopen the app.

How do I change my name or avatar?

Open "Player" from the main menu: you can update your name and photo at any time, even during a game.

How do I request deletion of my data?

Email us at the address above with the player name you used: we'll delete your data within a few business days. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Are the ads shown to children appropriate?

Ads in Wut?! are configured for general audiences ("G" rating) with no personalized tracking.
