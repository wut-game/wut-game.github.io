Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Wut?! is a local/online multiplayer party game. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can control it. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Information we collect

  • Device identifier — a random ID generated on your device, used to identify you within a game room. It is not linked to your real name or email.
  • Nickname and avatar — the name and photo you choose to set in the Player screen, shown to other players in your game room. Avatar photos you upload are stored on our server (Supabase) so other players in your room can see them.
  • Gameplay data — room codes, scores and in-match answers, used only to run the game and sync it in real time between players in the same room.
  • Local-only statistics — your personal "Hall of Shame Archive" stats are stored exclusively on your device and are never sent to our servers or to other players.
  • Camera / Photo Library access — requested only if you choose to take or pick a photo for your avatar. We don't access your camera or photos for any other purpose.
  • Game Center (iOS) / Play Games (Android) — if you choose to sign in, your public gamer name/avatar may be used to pre-fill your profile. This is handled by Apple/Google under their own privacy policies; we don't receive your Apple ID or Google account details.

Voice chat

During a match you can talk to the other players in your room with push-to-talk: the microphone is only on while you hold the talk button, and each message lasts at most 10 seconds. The voice chat closes shortly after the match ends. You can turn it off at any time from the voice chat menu.

  • Microphone — the app asks for microphone permission the first time you use voice chat. We use it only while you hold the talk button.
  • How audio travels — audio is sent directly between the devices of the players in the room (WebRTC). When a direct connection is not possible, it goes through a relay server operated by Cloudflare. We do not store voice recordings. Because of how direct connections work, the other players' devices may see your IP address while you are connected.
  • Reports and blocks — you can mute, block or report another player. Reports are stored on our server (Supabase) together with the device identifiers of the reporter and of the reported player. A player who receives 5 reports from different players cannot use the microphone for 30 days; the ban and the related reports are deleted after that period, and reports older than 30 days are deleted. Your list of blocked players is stored only on your device.

See also our Community rules.

Advertising

Wut?! shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads. Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) we ask for your consent through a consent form before showing personalized ads. On your device you can reset or limit the advertising ID at any time in the system settings.

Third-party services

  • Supabase — our backend/database provider, used to sync game rooms and store avatars and gameplay data described above.
  • Google AdMob — ad delivery, as described above.
  • Cloudflare (Realtime TURN) — relay servers used to connect voice chat when players cannot connect directly. Audio passing through the relay is not stored by us.

Data retention and deletion

Game room data is temporary and tied to the lifetime of a match. Your nickname and avatar persist on our server until you request deletion. To request deletion of any data associated with your device, email us at wutgame.support@gmail.com.

Age requirement

Wut?! is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you think a child under 13 is using the app, contact us and we will delete the related data. We do not require an account, email address, or any other personal contact information to use the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about voice chat? Email wutgame.support@gmail.com.

Um jogador que receba 5 denúncias de jogadores diferentes não pode usar o microfone durante 30 dias; o bloqueio e as denúncias associadas são eliminados após esse período, e as denúncias com mais de 30 dias são eliminadas. A tua lista de jogadores bloqueados é guardada apenas no teu dispositivo.

Consulta também as nossas Regras da comunidade.

Publicidade

O Wut?! mostra anúncios através do Google AdMob. O AdMob pode usar o ID de publicidade do teu dispositivo e dados semelhantes para mostrar e medir anúncios. Quando é obrigatório (EEE, Reino Unido, Suíça), pedimos o teu consentimento através de um formulário antes de mostrar anúncios personalizados. Nas definições do dispositivo podes repor ou limitar o ID de publicidade a qualquer momento.

Serviços de terceiros

  • Supabase — o nosso fornecedor de backend/base de dados, usado para sincronizar as salas de jogo e guardar os avatares e os dados de jogo descritos acima.
  • Google AdMob — entrega de anúncios, como descrito acima.
  • Cloudflare (Realtime TURN) — servidores de retransmissão usados para ligar o chat de voz quando os jogadores não se conseguem ligar diretamente. O áudio que passa pelo servidor não é guardado por nós.

Conservação e eliminação de dados

Os dados das salas de jogo são temporários e estão ligados à duração de uma partida. A tua alcunha e o teu avatar permanecem no nosso servidor até pedires a sua eliminação. Para pedir a eliminação de quaisquer dados associados ao teu dispositivo, escreve-nos para wutgame.support@gmail.com.

Idade mínima

O Wut?! destina-se a utilizadores com 13 anos ou mais e não se dirige a crianças com menos de 13 anos. Não recolhemos conscientemente dados pessoais de crianças com menos de 13 anos; se achas que uma criança com menos de 13 anos usa a aplicação, escreve-nos e apagaremos os dados associados. Não exigimos conta, endereço de e-mail nem quaisquer outros dados de contacto pessoais para usar a aplicação.
